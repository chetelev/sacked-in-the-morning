# This week's verdicts (site/index.html)

Scope: the whole static site, opened on "This week's verdicts". Mode: Read. Built code-led (no image generation on this machine).

Audience and job: football fans on phones, arriving from Reddit or a chat link, who want to know in seconds who r/PremierLeague and r/FantasyPL are turning on or loving this gameweek, see the comments behind it, and share it. Carries the four kept screens: player page, clubs, week by week, team of the week; plus the full ranked table below the splash.

Constraints: PRODUCT.md. Red is sell or hated, yellow is hold, green is buy or loved (owner-pinned). No eyebrow labels; credits sit under headlines as bylines.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every gameweek is a tabloid back page: the most-slated player gets the splash, everyone else an inside-page strap. It refuses the stats-app arrangement of cards, avatars and mini bars.

OWN-WORLD: Cool newsprint ground, black ink, thick black rules and halftone dot fields. One tabloid red owns the masthead and every hated or sell verdict; green only for loved and buy; yellow only for hold and the LIVE flash. Heavy condensed caps for headlines, a sturdy news serif for decks and body, narrow grotesk caps for straps, bylines and figures. Clubs appear as plain jerseys in home colours.

STORY: A fan sees who the subreddits are turning on this week, reads the comments that prove it, then screenshots or shares the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 wide. Red masthead with the name and a yellow LIVE GW flash; a strap with both subreddits and the update time; the splash: a pun headline at about 90px naming the most-hated player over a halftone field, a byline crediting the subreddit and comment count, a one-line deck, the share in big red figures; three inside straps (most loved, most sold, most bought) with verdict pills; a black section bar fixed at the bottom.

FORM: Tabloid back page, first on the ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the owner); seed key 3bac36d1. Signature move: headlines are written from the verdict, a new pun each gameweek, and every page of the season is kept as a back page.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
